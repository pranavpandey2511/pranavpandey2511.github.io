**Role:** Co-founder & CTO · **Status:** 2023 – 2025

Stybe was an AI-powered fashion e-commerce startup where I owned the entire AI and engineering stack — architecture, backend, and ML.

## What I built

- **Production RAG system** (LLMs + vector databases) serving as an AI personal stylist for personalized fashion discovery.
- **GenAI conversational search** with custom RAG and hybrid retrieval (sparse BM25/BM42 + dense embeddings) enabling natural-language product discovery.
- **Cross-platform product matching** at **86%+ accuracy** using semantic similarity and vector search (Qdrant), powering a price-comparison engine.
- **LLM-based product tagging** (GPT-3.5) and a cold-start solution via an AI-driven implicit/explicit feedback loop for continuous personalization.

**Tech:** Python · OpenAI GPT · Qdrant · Elasticsearch · FastAPI · SQL · Docker · NLP · Computer Vision
