**Code:** [github.com/pranavpandey2511/PROBA-V-Super-Resolution](https://github.com/pranavpandey2511/PROBA-V-Super-Resolution)

Work on the **PROBA-V super-resolution challenge**: reconstructing high-resolution satellite images from multiple low-resolution captures of the same scene, using imagery from ESA's PROBA-V Earth-observation mission. Unlike classic single-image super-resolution, the multi-frame setting lets a model exploit sub-pixel shifts between captures — but real satellite data brings cloud cover, registration errors, and brightness variation that synthetic benchmarks don't have.

The [repository](https://github.com/pranavpandey2511/PROBA-V-Super-Resolution) contains the full report and implementation.
